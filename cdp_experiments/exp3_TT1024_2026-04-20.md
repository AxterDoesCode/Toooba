## Verdict
**Net negative, consistent with user guidance "don't grow table sizes".**

- Patricia regressed +3.2%. Useful dropped 16% (1762 → 1484) despite bigger TT.
- Health's issued count jumped 23% (23274 → 28584) but useful stayed flat and
  accuracy fell 28.7% → 23.2%. Bigger TT kept more stale / spurious
  (vaddr, pcHash, relOffset) triples alive; on miss these reanimated false
  correlations that fired useless prefetches.
- Other benchmarks effectively flat.

## Insight
Growing the TT doesn't just "reduce aliasing" — it also retains more noisy
patterns that get reinforced and cause bad prefetches. The mechanism itself
can't distinguish genuine-long-range from spurious retained entries.

Algorithmic improvements are needed, not size growth. Directions left to try:

- **Path history indexing (SPP-style)**: concatenate/XOR last-K pcHashes as the
  PC-table signature, capturing *context* rather than raw-PC identity. Create a
  new variant `mkCDPStatefulRelativePathHist` for comparison (per plan.md
  guidance — new variants for vastly different mechanisms).
- **Multi-offset issue**: emit up to K high-conf offsets per decision, not just
  the single best. Direct coverage increase for PCs with multiple children.
- **Lookahead prefetch chaining**: after issuing one prefetch, use the predicted
  candidate as a *new* virtual miss address and recurse for another level.
- **Stride + CDP hybrid**: combine stride prefetching on demand misses with CDP's
  content-scan on the prefetched line.
