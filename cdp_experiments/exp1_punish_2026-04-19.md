# Exp 1: Useless-prefetch provenance + confidence decrement (2026-04-19)

## Change
Attribute each useless prefetch (reportEviction while wasPrefetch=True) to its
trainer (pcHash, relOffsetIdx) and decrement that single confidence slot by 1.
Filter entry extended to carry provenance (no extra BRAM). Neighbour-chain
prefetches marked non-punishable (no clean attribution).

## Verdict
**Net negative.** Patricia slowed 4.5%, other benchmarks essentially flat.
Accuracy went up on 3, down on 2.

## Analysis
Useless-eviction signal is noisier than assumed. A "useless" prefetch can be:
  (a) Pattern-wrong (correct to punish).
  (b) Correct pattern but timing-wrong — line arrived early, was evicted
      under cache pressure before demand touched it.
Punishing (b) destroys good patterns. For patricia (tight pointer-chase
timing) we likely punished many (b) cases: issued count dropped 15589 → 14415
(-8%) AND useful dropped 1762 → 1244 (-29%). So we killed good patterns.

## Next directions to try
- Exp 2: raise confidenceThreshold 1 → 2 (no punish). Forces a pattern to be
  confirmed twice before prefetching. Baseline threshold=1 is very aggressive.
- Exp 3: probabilistic punish (50%) — halve the effective decrement magnitude.
- Exp 4: grow confidence to Bit#(4) + threshold=2 — more headroom between
  reinforcement and punishment.
- Exp 5: punish only on a SECOND eviction of the same (pcHash, relOffsetIdx)
  within a window — requires a small shadow counter table.
