## Variant

H14 = H7 with `decayInterval = 128` (was 256). Hardware-zero param change.

## Hypothesis

The 2026-04-20 memory note showed `16 → 64` was +2.65% on a 5-bench
H4-era pipeline. We've never re-swept on H7. Faster decay (128 vs 256)
should:
- Counter-saturation: refresh stuck `(uf=15, us=15)` deadlock more often,
  letting the kill-switch gate fire on bad PCs
- Adversely: also decay good PCs' conf faster, possibly hurting
  high-conf PCs (84e0 in patricia, 92a0/91ee/91f8 in treeadd)

## Interpretation

Faster decay hurts the **good PCs more than it suppresses bad PCs**:

- **Patricia (+3,807)**: PC 84e0 dominates. Its conf entries decay faster,
  occasionally dropping below `confidenceThreshold=1` between hot
  prefetch bursts → some prefetches don't issue → fewer DRAM-bypass
  events. The hypothesis that faster decay would help by re-firing the
  kill-switch on bad PCs doesn't apply here — patricia barely has bad
  PCs to suppress.
- **Health (+7,817)**: similar — multiple hot PCs (93d4, 943e, 9374)
  see conf decay nipping their high-confidence offsets. The kill-switch
  gate doesn't fire on them under H7 either.
- **Treeadd (+1,355)**: good PCs (92a0/91ee/91f8) lose conf faster too;
  bad PCs (9094/909a/9082) don't get further suppressed because they're
  already saturated at us=uf=15.

The 2026-04-20 finding (16→64 was +2.65%) was on a much-coarser pipeline
that was decay-starved. Once the kill-switch + overflow-bypass
structural fixes were applied, decay=256 lands near the sweet spot.
**128 is too fast.**

## Decision

H14 dropped. **decayInterval=256 (H7's current value) is at or near
optimal** on the modern pipeline. Further sweeps not worth the rebuild
time.

## Memory update

`cdp_insight_decay_interval.md` should note that the optimal decay
interval depends on the underlying pipeline structure — pre-H4 era
favored 64, post-H7 era favors 256.
