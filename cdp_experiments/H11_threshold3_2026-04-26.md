Date: 2026-04-26
Source: `Prefetcher_top.bsv` (DATA_PREFETCHER_CDP_KILLSWITCH_H11 branch, reuses CDPKillSwitchH7 module)
Logs: `/local/scratch/ac2822/NewTooobaLogs/variantH11_threshold3_2026-04-26/`

## Variant

H11 = H7 with `confidenceThreshold = 3` (was 1). All other parameters and
mechanics identical: 4-bit kill-switch counters, 3× useless amplification,
1024-entry pcTable, 1024-entry prefetchFilter, overflow-bypass incomingQ.

## Hypothesis (from H10 per-PC analysis)

- **Treeadd**: 3 polluting PCs (9094 / 909a / 9082) issue 8,941 prefetches
  at 5.6% useful rate. With threshold=3, they cannot accumulate enough
  conf to fire → suppressed → less pipeline pollution.
- **Patricia**: PC 84e0 has 96.5% accuracy and many useful events. Should
  saturate conf fast → still issues at threshold=3.
- **Health**: 5+ hot PCs with 11-62% accuracy. Most should still reach
  conf=3 if they have enough events.
- **Risk**: bench-mix-sensitive — multi-PC benches with *thin* training
  per PC may lose tail useful events.

## Per-bench interpretation

### Treeadd (validated): −391 cyc

Predicted suppression of polluting PCs works. The 3 bad PCs (9094 / 909a
/ 9082) each had ~5.6% useful rate; their conf accumulator stays low
under threshold=3 so they stop issuing. Result: less pipeline pollution
overhead. Effect is small (−0.070%) because the LLC-shuffle benefit per
treeadd useful is only ~10 cyc.

### em3d (bonus): −549 cyc

Em3d had 2.2% useful rate across all PCs (essentially noise). Threshold=3
silences most of them. The minor cycle improvement comes from removing
the back-pressure overhead of useless prefetches, not from any newly-
captured useful events.

### Health (regression): +7,992 cyc

H10's per-PC table showed health has 5+ hot PCs (93d4, 943e, 9374, 9406,
9414) each with 11-62% useful rate but moderate event counts. With
threshold=3:
- PCs with **short-lead useful events** (943e: median leadTime=17,
  9374: median 16) accumulate conf slowly because their useful events
  fire close to demand timing — not enough time gap to complete the
  Training-trigger TT lookup before the next demand.
- Result: tail PCs lose their conf entries before they fire, fewer
  useful prefetches, more demand misses.

Health's 2,215 timely useful drops to ~1,500-1,800 (estimated from cycle
delta of +7,992 ≈ 800 lost timely × ~10 cyc).

### Patricia (regression): +11,402 cyc

The most surprising result. Patricia is dominated by PC 84e0 (96.5%
accuracy, 1,408 useful events) which clearly DOES reach conf=3.

Hypothesis: the regression comes from **non-84e0 PCs that contributed
DRAM-bypass prefetches**. H10 showed patricia's `prefetchMissLL = 1,456`
(every prefetch went to DRAM, structural). 84e0 alone issues only
1,459 prefetches. The remaining ~700 prefetches from other PCs (9054,
9078, 9056, 9330, etc.) — even at low useful rates — also went to DRAM
and contributed real DRAM-bypass savings.

Threshold=3 silences these tail PCs → 11,402 cyc lost ≈ 110 lost
DRAM-bypass events × 100 cyc each. Roughly matches our 38% coverage
estimate from earlier (`cdp_insight_dram_bypass_unique_to_patricia.md`).

**This is an important finding:** patricia's 38% DRAM-coverage ISN'T
all from 84e0; some comes from low-accuracy tail PCs that nevertheless
do trigger DRAM-bypass when they hit. Suppressing them sacrifices real
gains.

### Bisort: +509 cyc (small regression)

Bisort's hot PC 93c2 had 86% accuracy with 291 useful events. Should
reach conf=3. But bisort's median leadTime is 19 cyc — short. The
tail of bisort PCs (9390, 93f0) had longer leadTimes and likely lost
conf accumulation under threshold=3.

## Why one-size-fits-all threshold can't win

The benchmarks have very different optimal thresholds:

| bench | optimal threshold | reason |
|:---|:---|:---|
| treeadd | high (3+) | strong good/bad PC dichotomy; bad PCs flood with low conf |
| patricia | low (1) | many tail PCs contribute DRAM-bypass at low conf |
| health | low (1-2) | multiple moderate-conf PCs need permission to fire |
| em3d | irrelevant | mostly noise, small absolute impact |
| bisort | low (1-2) | tight loop, conf accumulates slowly |

A **per-PC adaptive threshold** would be needed to capture treeadd's
gain without losing patricia/health. The kill-switch's `us > uf` gate
is already a per-PC filter, but it operates on a different signal
(useful-vs-useless ratio) than confidence accumulation.

## Decision: skip H12 (threshold=5)

H12 (threshold=5) would be even MORE aggressive. Patricia and health
would regress further (more tail PCs silenced). Treeadd improvement
might grow slightly but the geomean regression would dominate.

**Don't run H12.** Threshold sweep is exhausted as an avenue.

## Next direction: LLC shrink

The remaining open question from the user: can we make health/treeadd
get DRAM-bypass via working set vs LLC sizing?

Current LLC = 1MB (CACHE_ALEX_SMALLISH). Memory note `cdp_insight_llc_resize`
showed 512KB had no effect on small benches. To push their working sets
out of LLC, we need significantly smaller LLC.

Plan:
1. Add `CACHE_ALEX_SMALLISH_TINYLL` to ProcConfig.bsv: L1=8KB (unchanged),
   LLC=128KB (LOG_LLC_LINES=11) or 64KB (LOG_LLC_LINES=10).
2. Whitelist `ALEX_SMALLISH_TINYLL` in Include_RISCY_Config.mk.
3. Build and bench H7 (the reference) at this config — confirms
   prefetchMissLL goes up on health/treeadd, indicating DRAM-bypass.
4. Compare cycle improvements vs default LLC.

If LLC=128KB shows DRAM-bypass on health/treeadd:
- Validates user's hypothesis structurally.
- Future H7 work focused on these benches now has DRAM-bypass headroom.

If LLC=128KB STILL doesn't push these out:
- These benches' working sets are smaller than 128KB.
- Need either even smaller LLC or workload grow.
