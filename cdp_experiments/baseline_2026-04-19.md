Observations:
- Accuracy is low everywhere; em3d is catastrophic (5.7%) while health is the best.
- Thousands of useless evictions carry a wasted signal: the PC+offset that caused each one could have its confidence decremented. Currently only random decay punishes, with no causal link.
- PC collisions at pcTableSize=1024 are rare (0 in health/patricia). Training table size is only 64 (2-way) — that's 128 entries total; expect some aliasing on larger working sets.
- filter HIT >> filter MISS everywhere: duplicate-drop mechanism is effective.
- No TLB exceptions anywhere.
