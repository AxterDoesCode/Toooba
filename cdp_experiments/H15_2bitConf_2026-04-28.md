# H15 — H7 with 2-bit confidence counters (BRAM shrink, step 1)

Date: 2026-04-28
Config: CACHE_ALEX_SMALLISH (1MB LLC), 8-bench dissertation set.
Logs: `/local/scratch/ac2822/NewTooobaLogs/variantH15_8bench_2026-04-28/`
Reference H7: `variantH7_overflowBypass_2026-04-22/`
Reference NoPref2: `variantNoPref2_postCapPtrFix_2026-04-22/`

## Mechanism

Strict superset of H7. Only change:

```bsv
typedef Vector#(15, Bit#(2)) PCRelOffsetConf2BitT;  // was Bit#(3)
```

Confidence counter saturates at 3 instead of 7. H7's default
`confidenceThreshold=1` (issue at any nonzero conf) is fully expressible
in 2 bits, so threshold stays at 1 — H15 keeps "issue if seen ≥1 time"
semantics. The only behavioural loss is finer-grained ranking when
multiple offsets in a PC entry are above threshold.

Implementation: `CDPKillSwitchH15.bsv`. New local types
`PCRelOffsetConf2BitT` and `PCTableAcc2BitEntryT` to avoid perturbing
shared `PCRelOffsetConfT` in `CDP.bsv`. Five `Bit#(3)` → `Bit#(2)`
edits in conf paths. Module renamed `mkCDPStatefulRelativeKillSwitchH15`.

## BRAM accounting

PC-Table-only change. Other tables unchanged.

| table | H7 entry width | H15 entry width | H7 total | H15 total |
|:---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Training Table (128 entries) | 85 b | 85 b | 1.36 KB | 1.36 KB |
| **PC Table (1024 entries)** | **70 b** | **55 b** | **8.96 KB** | **7.04 KB** |
| Prefetch Filter (1024 entries) | 76 b | 76 b | 9.73 KB | 9.73 KB |
| **Total CDP BRAM** | — | — | **20.05 KB** | **18.13 KB** |

PC entry composition: tag(1) + pcHash(16) + 15 × conf + uf(4) + us(4).
- H7: 1 + 16 + 15·3 + 4 + 4 = 70 bits
- H15: 1 + 16 + 15·**2** + 4 + 4 = 55 bits

**Net BRAM saving: 1.92 KB (9.6% of CDP BRAM).**

## Why health alone regresses

Health is the densest pointer-chase outside patricia and the only bench
that produces enough per-PC training events to actually exercise the
3-bit conf range. Parser data:

| metric | H7 health | H15 health | Δ |
|:---|---:|---:|---:|
| prefetch issued | 12,240 | 11,886 | −354 |
| prefetchHit (redundant) | 6,350 | 5,850 | −500 |
| prefetchMiss | 4,342 | 4,465 | +123 |
| usefulPrefetch | 3,332 | 3,422 | **+90** |
| uselessPrefetch | 1,010 | 1,043 | **+33** |
| latePrefetch | 1,120 | 1,156 | +36 |
| lateUsefulPrefetch | 1,117 | 1,150 | +33 |
| **timely useful** | 2,215 | 2,272 | +57 |

H15 actually issues *more* useful and *fewer* redundant — the conf
saturation arrives faster, so more PCs cross threshold sooner. But
useless also rises (+33), and at health's near-zero `prefetchMissLL=1`
the savings per useful are LLC-hit-only (~5 cyc each), so the
+33 useless × ~LLC-pollution outweighs +57 timely useful. Net cycle
cost +2,947 / +0.236%.

This is a real but minor mechanism: with 2-bit conf, the per-PC ranking
across the 15 offsets is coarser, so when the gate picks `bestVecIdx`
under multiple-saturated, the chosen offset is sometimes less optimal.

## Patricia — the big win is unchanged

Patricia's −12.06% DRAM-bypass speedup is **identical** in H15
(−12.0616% vs H7's −12.0612%). The 84e0 dominant-PC story doesn't
need finer conf differentiation than 2 bits — the offset is
overwhelmingly stable.

## Verdict

**H15 is a clean BRAM win.** 1.92 KB saved / 9.6% of CDP BRAM, at a
cost of 0.029 pp geomean (well under the 0.1 pp noise floor for
inter-run variation, and concentrated entirely on health).

Recommended: adopt 2-bit conf as the default for further BRAM-shrink
sweeps. Next candidates per the BRAM accounting table:

1. **Filter `lineAddr` (58 bits × 1024 = 7.25 KB)** — biggest single
   contributor. Could swap for a hash tag (e.g. 16-bit) and accept some
   collision-driven attribution loss. Sweep: H16 = H15 + 16-bit
   filter tag.
2. **Filter `pcHash` (16 bits × 1024 = 2 KB)** — currently duplicated
   from PC table. Could narrow to 8 or 12 bits.
3. **Training table `storedVaddr` (64 bits × 128 = 1 KB)** — tag could
   shrink to a hash to ~16-24 bits for ~6-7 KB savings; risk is
   spurious tag matches creating phantom training pairs.
4. **PC entry `usefulCount` / `uselessCount` (4 bits each)** — unused
   above ~7-8 in practice for the 8-bench set; could narrow to 3 bits.
